Bridesmaid dresses

Mixed but coordinated. Everyone picks her own dress inside the palette — the three of you should land in different colours or shapes, not the same dress. Jenni’s own notes are in quotes on the cards. Nothing here is mandatory; these are starting points, and the links go to the live product pages.

Theme

First day of spring. California flower energy, Monet’s garden, wildflowers.

Colour

Wine/berry, green, purple — or a pastel: blue, lilac, sage, butter. Same family is great, same dress is not.

Texture

Silk, satin, velvet, lace, jacquard, chiffon, florals… Floor-length ideally; the two midis are marked.

Send Jenni your pick so nobody doubles up. Watch the “made to order” and “final sale” tags — those can’t come back.

Ordering notes

  • Timing: the wedding is Saturday 20 March 2027. Jenny Yoo made-to-order takes ~8 weeks, plus 3–4 weeks for alterations — so order by early January; December is safe. RIXO Fern is a pre-order shipping 17 December.
  • Returns: Reformation and BHLDN/Anthropologie are returnable. Jenny Yoo made-to-order and Amsale are final sale — order a swatch first if unsure (Amsale swatches are $5).
  • Colours shown on Amsale and Jenny Yoo Nicole are defaults — every one of them comes in burgundy, navy, black and greens.
  • Weather: March in New York averages around 43°F. A wrap, cape or long sleeve is worth thinking about for the photos outside.
